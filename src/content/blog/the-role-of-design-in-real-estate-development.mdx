#### Where motorisation makes the biggest difference

High windows, large glass doors and groups of blinds become easier to manage when they move together. Motorisation can also support a clean, cord-free appearance.

#### Choose control that suits your routine

Depending on the system, blinds may be controlled by a remote, wall switch, app or scheduled routine. Think about who uses the room and how often the setting needs to change.

#### Plan power and placement early

Battery and wired options suit different windows and renovation stages. Discuss access, charging, motor position and fabric weight before ordering so the finished system feels considered.
