#### Living rooms and street-facing windows

Tilting the blades lets you direct daylight while limiting outside views. This makes shutters useful where you want privacy without making the room feel closed in.

#### Bedrooms and quieter spaces

Shutters provide dependable coverage and a structured finish. If near-darkness is essential, ask how the frame and blade design will manage light around the edges.

#### Kitchens and practical areas

Easy-care materials are helpful where moisture, cooking and frequent cleaning are part of daily life. The correct material should be selected for the room conditions.
