#### When blockout is the better choice

Blockout fabrics suit spaces where sleep, screen viewing or stronger privacy matters. They reduce incoming light through the fabric, although some light may still appear around the edges.

#### When light filtering works beautifully

Light-filtering fabrics diffuse harsh daylight and keep a room feeling bright. They are well suited to living rooms, kitchens and areas where atmosphere matters more than darkness.

#### Layering gives you more control

Pairing a filtering layer with a blockout option lets the window adapt from morning to night. It can also add depth and softness to the room.
