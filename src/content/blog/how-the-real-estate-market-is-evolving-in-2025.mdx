#### Decide how you want the room to feel

Sheer curtains bring softness, height and movement to a room. Light-filtering blinds create a streamlined finish that sits close to the window and works well in compact spaces.

#### Think about privacy throughout the day

Both options soften the view from outside during daylight, but privacy changes after dark when indoor lights are on. For bedrooms or street-facing rooms, consider pairing a sheer layer with a blockout curtain or blind.

#### Consider how the window is used

Sliding doors often benefit from a curtain that stacks clear of the opening. Roller blinds can be an efficient choice for individual windows, desks and busy living areas.
