#### Choosing from a small sample alone

Fabric can look different across a full window and as light changes. View colours in the room, beside the walls, flooring and furniture whenever possible.

#### Focusing only on appearance

A beautiful finish still needs to manage privacy, glare, heat and daily use. Write down the practical job for each window before comparing styles.

#### Missing the small details

Handles, window winders, doors, skirting and furniture can affect operation. Professional measuring helps identify obstacles before the product is made.

#### Treating every room the same

Bedrooms, living rooms and wet areas have different needs. One consistent colour can still be used across several product types to keep the home connected.
