#### Start with the direction of the light

North- and west-facing windows can receive stronger heat and glare, while other rooms may need gentle filtering rather than full blockout. Notice when the room feels hottest and where reflections become uncomfortable.

> Choose for the room first. Colour and finish become much easier once the practical job is clear.

#### Match the fabric to the room

Sunscreen fabrics can reduce glare while keeping a view. Light-filtering fabrics soften daylight and add daytime privacy. Blockout blinds are a stronger choice for bedrooms, nurseries and media rooms.

#### Do not overlook the fit

Small gaps, oversized rolls and the wrong mounting position can affect both comfort and appearance. Professional measuring helps the blind sit neatly and perform as intended.
